No sign-in or account is needed; the app is anonymous.

To see a fully populated event:
1. On the first screen, tap the search field ("Event code or name"), type DEMO and tap Search on the keyboard.
   (Or open https://attnd.app/join/DEMO/ on the device: it opens the app, or the App Clip, straight into the event.)
2. The demo event is always happening now, so the map, schedule (now / next), locations and updates are all filled in.

Location permission is optional: the first screen asks for it to list nearby events, and it is used to show your position (blue dot) on the event map. Declining it does not limit anything else.
Notifications are optional: they can be turned on in Options (the gear button at the top of each event screen) and deliver announcements from the event's organizer.

To switch events, use Options (gear button) > Leave Event.
