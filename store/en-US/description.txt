Attnd is the easiest way to follow a live event — a festival, a conference, a fair or a market — from the moment you arrive.

SCAN AND YOU'RE IN
Scan the QR code at the event with your camera. Attnd opens right away as an App Clip, so there's nothing to install first. You can also search for the event's code or name, or find events happening near you.

THE WHOLE EVENT ON ONE MAP
Every stage, vendor, food stand, info booth and restroom the organizer has added, pinned on a map. Tap a pin for details and walking directions.

WHAT'S ON NOW AND NEXT
The schedule shows what's happening right now and what's coming up, with countdowns, in the event's own time zone, laid out the way the organizer chooses (timeline, by day or by location), with search to find something specific.

UPDATES FROM THE ORGANIZER
Stage change? Set running late? Organizer announcements appear in Updates, and you can turn on notifications to get them as they're sent.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
When organizers edit the map or the schedule, Attnd follows along within seconds — no refreshing, no outdated PDFs.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no sign-in. Attnd doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. Location access is optional and only used to find nearby events and show your position on the map. Notifications are optional too.

MADE FOR EVERY IPHONE
Attnd adapts to every iPhone, including iPhone Duo: unfold it to see the map and more side by side.

Organizing an event? Create your event's map, schedule and updates at attnd.app.
