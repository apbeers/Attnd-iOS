No sign-in or account is needed; the app is anonymous.

To see a fully populated event:
1. On the first screen, tap the search field ("Event code or name"), type DEMO and tap Search on the keyboard.
   (Or open https://attnd.app/join/DEMO/ on the device: it opens the app, or the App Clip, straight into the event.)
2. The demo event is always happening now, so the map, schedule (now / next), locations and updates are all filled in.

Location permission is optional: the first screen asks for it to list nearby events, and it is used to show your position (blue dot) on the event map. Declining it does not limit anything else.
Notifications are optional: they can be turned on in Options (the gear button at the top of each event screen) and deliver announcements from the event's organizer.

After joining an event for the first time, a short tour (onboarding cards) explains the map, schedule, updates, search and hearts and offers the optional location and notification prompts; it can be skipped with Skip and reopened any time from Options (gear button) > Tour. On devices with Apple Intelligence it also shows "Plan your day" examples that open Ask, answered on the device.

Follow on Lock Screen (Live Activity): heart a schedule item (heart button on any schedule row), or turn on Options > Lock Screen > Follow on Lock Screen and tap Start Now. A Live Activity then shows what is on now and next on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. With notifications on, hearted items also get a reminder 15 minutes before they start. "Tell Me When I Arrive" (optional, asks for Always location) only notices arrival at the event's venue and never leaves the device.
Urgent announcements from organizers (e.g. a cancellation) are delivered as time-sensitive notifications and shown on the Live Activity.

Diagnostics: crash and hang reports from MetricKit and anonymous performance counts (launch and loading times in ranges) are sent without any identifier and are not linked to the user or used for tracking. Options > About & Support > "Turn On Debug Logging for 10 Minutes" is optional and user-started: for 10 minutes it sends detailed app logs (no search text, location or account details, no device identifier) under a random debug code the user can share with support; it turns itself off. "Recent Logs" only shows the logs kept on the device.

To switch events, use Options (gear button) > Leave Event.
